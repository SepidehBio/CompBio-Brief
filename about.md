    <h2>What this is</h2>

    <p>
      The site brings together concise research notes, tool references,
      public datasets, tutorials and practical workflows covering common
      tasks in computational biology.
    </p>

    <p>
      The aim is to make technical information easier to understand,
      reproduce and use. The writing is deliberately clear and practical,
      with attention to the reasoning behind analytical choices rather
      than jargon for its own sake.
    </p>

    <h2>Why it exists</h2>

    <p>
      Computational biology involves many small decisions: how data are
      divided, how missing values are handled, which quality-control
      steps are appropriate, how a method is evaluated, and how a
      workflow can be reproduced.
    </p>

    <p>
      These decisions are often scattered across papers, documentation,
      notebooks and project repositories. CompBio Brief brings selected
      knowledge and practical guidance together in one accessible resource.
    </p>

    <p>
      The notes are written to be useful to researchers, students and
      computational scientists who want a concise starting point before
      going deeper into the literature or documentation.
    </p>

    <h2>Scope</h2>

    <p>Topics include:</p>

    <ul>
      <li>Computational biology and bioinformatics</li>
      <li>Biomedical machine learning</li>
      <li>Single-cell analysis</li>
      <li>Spatial transcriptomics</li>
      <li>Variant interpretation</li>
      <li>Sequence analysis</li>
      <li>Biomedical datasets</li>
      <li>Scientific Python and research software</li>
      <li>Reproducible computational workflows</li>
    </ul>

    <p>
      The site focuses on practical and technically grounded material.
      Topics are included when there is sufficient understanding or
      reliable documentation to explain them accurately.
    </p>

    <h2>How it is built</h2>

    <p>
      CompBio Brief is built from scratch using Jekyll, HTML, CSS and
      JavaScript. It uses no frontend framework or CSS library.
    </p>

    <p>
      The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and supports English, Danish
      and Persian, including automatic language detection and
      right-to-left text support.
    </p>

    <p>
      It also includes light and dark themes, responsive layouts and
      accessibility-oriented features.
    </p>
